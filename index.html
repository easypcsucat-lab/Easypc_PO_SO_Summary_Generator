How each status is decided
StatusRule (checked in this order)
CompletedPO QTY is more than 0 and PO QTY = SO QTY = DELIVERED = RECEIVED.
VarianceQTY VARIANCE (RECEIVED minus DELIVERED) is not 0, and either there is no delivery date or the days since delivery are past the SLA.
Expected Delivery TodayQTY VARIANCE is not 0 and the days since delivery equal the SLA.
Open Purchase OrderQTY VARIANCE is not 0 and the days since delivery are still within the SLA.
PendingNothing delivered yet and no delivery date.
(blank)None of the above.

Lines are grouped by PO. Quantities add up the lines with that PO and its SO#. The delivery date is taken from the first line that has a delivery reference, for example "PMW/OUT/71067 - 10/26/2026 02:05:45". Days since delivery are counted to today (Manila time). SLA: 2 days from a warehouse origin, 3 days from a store origin. An origin ending in "Store" counts as a store; any other origin, such as TECH IT EASY VENTURES INC. or TECHNEXUS, counts as a warehouse. Remarks are kept in this browser only, per PO#, and go into the export.

Source: the Odoo SO/PO export you upload. Figures are as accurate as that export; statuses are calculated as of the date you set above.
